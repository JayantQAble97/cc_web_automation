# TripStacc Home Page

What the TripStacc home page shows after login, observed on IDFC UAT (`https://uat-travel-idfc.travel-loyalty.com/`), desktop, on 2026-10-15. After login the page opens on the **Flights** tab by default.

## 1. Header

- Logo (links to the FIRST Rewards portal)
- **Flights** tab
- **Hotels** tab
- Profile icon showing the user's initials (for example "DK"), linking to My Account

## 2. Banner

- Full-width image carousel with several slides
- Slide indicator dots
- **Previous** and **Next** arrows (Flights tab only; the Hotels banner has no arrows)

## 3. Search widget

### Flights tab

- Trip type: **One way** / **Round trip**
- **From**: city or airport
- **To**: city or airport
- **Departure** date
- **Return** date
- **Travellers & Cabin Class** (for example "1 Traveller · Economy")
- **Search flights** button

### Hotels tab

- **Location**: search for a city or hotel
- **Check-in** date
- **Check-out** date
- **Rooms & guests** (for example "2 Rooms · 4 Adults · 2 Children")
- **Search Hotels** button

### Recent Searches

- Shown only when the user has searched before
- Cards for previous searches, for example "BOM → BLR · 17 Oct · 1 Traveller" for flights, or the city name for hotels

## 4. My Trips & Bookings

- "My Trips & Bookings" bar with a **Manage** link to upcoming bookings

## 5. Best offer for you

A row of offer cards. The offers differ between the Flights and Hotels tabs.

| Flights tab | Hotels tab |
|---|---|
| Flat ₹500 off (Diamond Reserve Credit Card, first flight above ₹5,000) | International Stay Deal (IDFC Debit Card) |
| Fly Abroad, Save ₹1,000 (IDFC Debit Card, international flight above ₹15,000) | Flat ₹1,000 off (first domestic hotel) |
| Flat ₹3,000 off (first international flight above ₹20,000) | Flat ₹3,000 off (first international hotel) |
| 5% Cashback on EMI (up to ₹1,500, no coupon code) | |

## 6. Exclusive Benefits on Credit Cards

- Image slider of credit card benefits
- "Please visit the **T&C** for details" link

## 7. Why book with FIRST Rewards Gallery?

Three benefits:

1. **Competitive Fares And Prices**: best prices for flights and hotels
2. **Bonus Reward Points On Hotels**: 20 bonus points per ₹100 spent on eligible IDFC FIRST cards
3. **Travel Anywhere In The World**: fly out or stay anywhere

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

- Category tabs: **General**, **Flights**, **Hotels**
- Expandable questions, for example:
  - What are the reasons for dynamic fares?
  - What travel documents should I carry during my trip?
  - How early should I check-in at the airport?
  - Can I book tickets for infants on Tripstacc?
  - Can I make a booking online for a child traveling alone?
